Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how weinnovate Design ("we", "us", or "our"), based in Adelaide, South Australia, handles information in connection with our website (weinnovate.design) and the software applications and tools we build and operate (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our Services:

  • Contact information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you contact us.
  • Usage data collected through standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), including pages visited, approximate location (city level), device type, and browser. This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.
  • Data accessed through connected accounts. Where one of our applications connects to a third-party service on your behalf (for example, Google services via OAuth), we access only the data scopes you explicitly authorise, and only to provide the functionality of the application.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our Services.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide client support.
  • To analyse aggregate usage of our website and applications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use data obtained through connected third-party accounts for advertising purposes.

3. Google API Services

Where our applications access Google APIs, our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data accessed via Google APIs is used only to provide the features of the relevant application.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the application's features, comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice.
  • Data is not used for serving advertisements.
  • Human access to this data is not permitted except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, to comply with the law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymised.

4. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information we hold, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and secure credential storage. Authentication tokens for connected services are stored securely and are never shared.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics purposes. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Links

Our Services may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Australian Privacy Act 1988), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact@weinnovate.design.
