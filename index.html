SEOApp — SEO reporting & analytics automation

Automated SEO reporting, powered by your own Google data

SEOApp is a reporting and analytics tool built and operated by weinnovate Design, a web technology consultancy based in Adelaide, South Australia. It connects to Google services you authorise and turns raw marketing data into clear, consolidated SEO reports for the websites we manage.

What SEOApp does

Analytics reporting

Reads website traffic data from Google Analytics (GA4) — sessions, users, conversions, channels — and compiles monthly performance reports.

Search performance

Reads Google Search Console data — queries, clicks, impressions, rankings — to track organic search visibility over time.

Ads performance

Reads Google Ads campaign data — spend, clicks, conversions, keywords — to report on paid search performance alongside organic results.

Consolidated dashboards

Combines all sources into a single HTML, PDF, or spreadsheet report per website or client — delivered by email or a private report portal.

How SEOApp uses your data

SEOApp requests access to Google user data solely to provide its reporting functionality:

  • Google Analytics & Search Console (read-only): to retrieve traffic and search performance metrics for report generation.
  • Google Ads: to retrieve campaign performance data for reporting, and — only where explicitly authorised by the account owner — to apply requested campaign optimisations.
  • Gmail (send): to deliver generated reports by email on your behalf.

Data is used only for these purposes. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including our adherence to the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.
